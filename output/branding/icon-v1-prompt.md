## Üretim istemi

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: a finished square mobile app icon for a grocery shopping checklist app.
Primary request: design one beautiful, original, understated logo that combines a grocery tote bag and a completed check mark in one unified symbol.
Composition: one large, precisely centered warm ivory tote-bag silhouette, with a generous dark forest-green check mark cut out of the bag body as negative space. The check mark is the same color as the background, not an extra floating badge. The handle is a short simple rounded arch with a clean open counter. The bag has softly rounded bottom corners, subtle outward shoulders, and a sturdy balanced silhouette. Make the geometry exceptionally clean and confident, like a carefully drawn identity mark. The check stays inside the body and has thick legs and softly rounded ends. The mark fills around 62 percent of the canvas width, with generous optical safe margins all around.
Style: flat, vector-like professional brand identity, crisp edges, minimal shapes, warm approachable everyday utility, strong recognizability at small icon sizes.
Palette: very deep forest teal green background, warm off-white symbol. Two solid colors only.
Canvas: 1024 by 1024 square, opaque full-bleed solid background extending to all four edges. This image itself is the icon artwork. Do not draw a rounded-square tile inside the image; no outer padding, no mockup canvas, no border.
Text: none.
Avoid: letters, words, initials, target bullseyes, red retail branding, extra list lines, groceries, leaves, stars, floating check badges, gradients, shadows, 3D, gloss, texture, perspective, tiny decorative details, watermarks, presentation sheets.
