## Üretim istemi

Use case: logo-brand, artistic reinterpretation of the supplied app icon.
Input image 1: the existing Target Bag app icon, used as the edit target and structural reference.

Art direction: a unified hand-painted emblem. The tote becomes the portrait's frame and the sitter's silhouette at once: a bold ivory arched tote handle above, broad curved shoulders and gently rounded bag base below. Inside that large silhouette, render a beautifully simplified yet unmistakable Mona Lisa, with center-parted dark hair and veil, calm direct gaze, the famous enigmatic closed-mouth smile, three-quarter seated shoulders and delicately crossed hands. Her dark draped gown flows naturally into the bag's lower shape, so portrait and shopping bag become one designed image. Use large elegant forms, not a tiny detailed painting pasted on an empty bag.
Integrate a single small but clear muted antique-gold check mark as a painterly flourish in the gown below her folded hands. The mark should feel like part of the composition, not a floating badge. Keep her face unobstructed and make the face the emotional focal point.
Style: warm Renaissance oil-painting sfumato, luminous amber skin, subtle visible brushwork, velvety olive shadows, refined illustrated museum identity, a delicate quiet wit. More artistic and soulful than the flat source icon, with confident simplified contours suitable for a phone icon. Avoid photorealism or a product photograph.
Composition: one centered oversized emblem, same overall balance and comfortable outer margins as the reference. The portrait occupies most of the bag body, with the ivory silhouette still readable around it. Very dark forest-green full-bleed background with a subtle painted surface. Warm ivory, deep green, umber, muted ochre and a restrained antique-gold accent.
Output: single square icon artwork, 1024x1024. No text, letters, wordmark, signature, outer frame, gallery setting, presentation sheet, phone mockup, 3D render, sparkle, collage, grocery props, or watermark. Do not include a rounded-square tile or outer white margin. Preserve the tasteful simplicity and visual weight of the original icon while making the artistic tribute clearly recognizable.
